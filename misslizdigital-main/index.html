About Miss Liz

Qualified Teacher

Miss Liz is a qualified teacher and phonics specialist with years of experience supporting children to build strong reading and writing skills. She is passionate about creating calm, structured and inclusive learning environments where every child feels safe, seen and empowered to succeed.

Committed to diversity and representation, Miss Liz proudly brings her identity as a Muslim educator who wears hijab into her teaching — ensuring children from all backgrounds feel respected and valued.

Beyond tutoring, Miss Liz is the self-published author of the children's book Jungle Kind, which celebrates kindness, courage and the beauty of nature. The book is designed to inspire young readers both at home and in the classroom.

Miss Liz Tutoring – Nurturing Phonics Skills & 11+ Success

Miss Liz is the official MP Ambassador, proudly representing a DfE-validated phonics scheme trusted in schools across the UK and internationally. Designed with inclusion at its core, Monster Phonics uses colour-coding and characters to support children with dyslexia, English as an additional language (EAL) and other diverse learning needs.

Services

ABC

Phonics Tutoring

(Reception to Year 2)

  • Systematic synthetic phonics (Phases 2–5)
  • Multisensory teaching methods to support all learners
  • Blending, segmenting, tricky words and fluency
  • Year 1 Phonics Screening Check preparation
  • Support for early readers, EAL learners and SEND

11+ Preparation

(Years 3 to 6)

We help children feel confident and capable in every aspect of 11+ English (grammar school and independent school routes).

  • Reading Comprehension: Strategies for retrieval, inference, vocabulary in context
  • Creative & Formal Writing: Structure, sentence variety, punctuation and vocabulary
  • SPaG: Grammar rules, sentence structures, punctuation, and spelling patterns
  • Verbal Reasoning: Vocabulary, logic, language patterns, and code-based tasks
  • Exam Strategy & Stamina: Time management, pacing and confidence building

Additional Subjects & Skills

  • Maths Support: Number, calculation, problem-solving, and 11+ Maths exam preparation
  • Non-Verbal Reasoning (NVR): Pattern recognition, logic, spatial awareness
  • Debate and Public Speaking: Building confident verbal communication and presentation skills

Online Tutoring

  • 1:1 and group sessions via Zoom
  • Engaging tools, visuals and bespoke resources
  • Regular parent updates and optional follow-up tasks

📃 Terms & Conditions

🛡️ Safeguarding

  • For the safety of all students, lessons may be recorded.
  • An adult (18+) must be present in the vicinity during online sessions.
  • Students must join lessons using their real name and keep their camera on unless otherwise advised by the teacher.

📚 Attendance & Equipment

  • Students should be seated in a quiet space with all necessary resources ready (e.g. paper, pencils, books).
  • If your child is unwell or cannot attend, please notify us as soon as possible.

💳 Payments & Cancellations

  • All lessons must be paid for in advance.
  • Lessons are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • To cancel or rearrange a 1:1 session, at least 48 hours' notice is required.
  • To withdraw from group tuition, a full term's written notice is required.

👩‍🏫 Teaching Staff

  • In the event of staff absence, a qualified member of the Miss Liz Tutoring team may lead the session.
